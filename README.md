# Bachelor Thesis
## Intro
This thesis was written at the conclusion of my Bachelor's degree at the University of Technology Graz (TUG) in cooperation with the Institute of Informatics (ITI).

The topic of the thesis is "Automotive Cybersecurity: Evaluation of Hacking and Service Tools Available for Automotive Communication". It is divided into three parts:
1. Introduction
2. Resources
3. Demonstration
